Scope And Approved Inputs

Approved Scope

Flat research/ scope for FormForge. The active ICP is client-service solo consultants, coaches, and small agencies doing client intake/onboarding.

Product Boundary

AI-assisted form builder prototype focused on describe, generate, edit, publish, submit, review, and export. No canonical synthesis has been written yet.

Approved inputs: research/competitive-analysis-porter-five-forces.md, research/competitive-analysis-swot.md, research/competitive-analysis-feature-pricing-matrix.md, and research/competitive-analysis-strategic-group-map.md.

Executive Synthesis

FormForge is entering a crowded form-builder market where generic form creation, conditional logic, file collection, analytics, integrations, and AI-assisted first drafts are already visible in mature competitors.

The clearest market gap is not "AI forms." It is a focused client-intake workflow that is deeper than a generic form builder but lighter than adopting a full client-management suite.

The gap remains a hypothesis, not a validated conclusion, because there are no target-buyer interviews, win/loss notes, live customer usage data, or hands-on competitor trials in the current evidence base.

GTM And Pricing Patterns

  1. Free and low-cost anchors dominate generic collection.
  2. AI creation is spreading across incumbents.
  3. Response limits, branding, custom domains, analytics, and automation are common upgrade levers.
  4. Integrations and handoff are a major battleground.
  5. Workflow depth raises willingness to pay.

Competitive Risks

  • Generic feature parity is high across logic, uploads, payments, analytics, branding, integrations, APIs, and AI.
  • Free substitutes are strong enough to delay paid adoption.
  • Suite gravity is real for buyers who want contracts, invoices, scheduling, portals, and automations.
  • FormForge's current handoff depth is thin relative to integration-heavy competitors.
  • Customer evidence is missing, so the main whitespace is still unvalidated.

Assumptions And Source Gaps

High Confidence

Active scope remains flat FormForge client-service intake; generic/free builder pressure is high; AI creation is table stakes.

Medium Confidence

Suite resistance, MCP/CLI willingness, and early integration requirements need customer interviews or trials.

  • No direct target-buyer interviews, win/loss notes, support tickets, or user analytics.
  • No hands-on competitor trials for AI quality, setup burden, integration friction, or client-facing polish.
  • Jotform exact public plan pricing and limits remain a source gap.
  • No review-site or community sentiment pass was included.
  • No direct test of Typeform MCP, Jotform APIs, Tally webhooks, Fillout API, or Paperform integrations.
