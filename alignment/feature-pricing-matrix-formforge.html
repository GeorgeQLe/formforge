Section feedback

Preliminary Synthesis Inputs

  1. The strongest pricing pressure comes from Tally, Fillout, forms.app, and Google Forms, not Typeform alone.
  2. The strongest packaging pressure comes from HoneyBook and Dubsado because they make intake part of a broader paid-client operating system.
  3. The strongest willingness-to-pay proof comes from specialized or workflow-rich intake, especially IntakeQ's forms, attachments, signatures, reminders, secure sharing, and exports.
  4. The most visible current FormForge gap is post-submission handoff/integrations.
  5. AI generation should be preserved as current product capability, but not treated as a standalone differentiator.

These are inputs for parent synthesis, not final recommendations.
