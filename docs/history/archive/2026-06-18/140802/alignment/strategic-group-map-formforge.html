Scope And Method

Scope

Flat research/ FormForge scope for client-service solo consultants, coaches, and small agencies doing client intake/onboarding.

FormForge's current product boundary remains the fresh-start AI-assisted form builder loop: describe, generate, edit visually, publish, receive submissions, and review/export responses.

Method

The map clusters competitors by buyer-visible packaging and adoption tradeoffs. It does not rank vendors, recommend positioning, or write canonical synthesis.

Source checks used public pages from Typeform, Jotform, Tally, Fillout, Google Forms, HoneyBook, Dubsado, IntakeQ, Paperform, forms.app, Typeform MCP, and Jotform developer docs.

Strategic Groups

Free Or Low-Friction Collectors

Google Forms, Tally, forms.app, and parts of Fillout compete on availability, generous free tiers, low setup burden, and good-enough form capability.

Synthesis input: this is the strongest substitute cluster and creates a low price ceiling for generic intake.

Polished General Form Platforms

Typeform, Paperform, Jotform, and Fillout compete on richer builder capability, brand polish, logic, analytics, payments, integrations, API/webhook surfaces, templates, and AI creation.

Synthesis input: this crowded middle weakens any "AI forms plus logic" differentiation claim.

Client-Service Operating Suites

HoneyBook and Dubsado own the surrounding client workflow: lead capture, portal, contracts, invoices, scheduling, workflows, automation, and reports.

Synthesis input: suites are threats for buyers who want one system and a possible gap signal for suite-resistant buyers.

Vertical Intake And Practice Workflow

IntakeQ competes on intake as a secure vertical workflow with reminders, signatures, attachments, document sharing, export, and compliance support.

Synthesis input: specialized intake can support higher WTP when operational depth is central.

Whitespace And Crowded Zones

Crowded Zones

  • Generic form creation and AI-assisted first drafts.
  • Low-cost basic collection.
  • Broad operational form platforms.
  • Full client-service workflow suites.

Whitespace Hypotheses

  • Client-intake-specific workflow without suite migration.
  • Post-submission handoff for service work.
  • Agent-operable intake handoff, but only if workflow-specific because Typeform MCP and Jotform APIs already exist.
